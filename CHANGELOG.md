# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

* Bounty board and challenge screens: countdowns to the daily and weekly reset above the Daily and Weekly columns.
* Quests menu, Bounties and Challenges tabs: both countdowns above the list.
* Vendors with limited stock: countdown to the daily restock above the vendor's items.
* Times come from the world's own reset schedule, not the PC clock.
* Countdowns show the two largest units (`5d 08h`, `13h 53m`, `42m 10s`), or every unit with Always Show Full Time.
* Checkboxes for each screen and for Always Show Full Time in Options > Gameplay.
